# Digital Transformation and Automation of a Procurement Logistics System

A graduation thesis focused on designing a comprehensive IT solution and business process architecture for **Golden House LLC** — a major developer with a portfolio exceeding 2 million square meters and 25 projects under construction simultaneously. The objective is a transition from decentralized manual procurement management to a unified digital ecosystem.

## Identified Problems (As-Is)

* **Patchwork automation** — manual transfer of specifications from BIM models to 1C generated 14.2% nomenclature errors and direct financial losses
* **Information gap** — the lack of a unified master information system (MSI) and decentralized accounting caused a logistical bullwhip effect with a coefficient of 2.89
* **Bureaucratization** — document approval cycles extended to 14 days due to paper-based workflows

## Developed Solutions (To-Be)

* **BIM → 1C:ERP integration** — design of a seamless connector for automatic upload of material quantities from the building's digital twin directly into the accounting database, eliminating human error
* **SRM and EDI implementation** — architecture for supplier interaction through a personal account with automatic prequalification based on state register databases
* **Physical logistics modernization** — design of a centralized hub and transition of on-site warehouses to a WMS with RFID tagging and intelligent time-slotting (TMS)

## Quantified Results

* Inventory error rate reduced from **14.2%** to the technological minimum (**< 0.5%**)
* Administrative document approval cycle shortened **by a factor of 4** — to 1.5 days
* Release of **61.86 billion UZS** in frozen working capital through excess inventory elimination
* Confirmed financial performance: **NPV of 1,620.0M UZS**, **IRR of 36.8%**, **discounted payback period (DPP) of 37 months**

## Methodology

The thesis applies financial and systems analysis methods: total cost of ownership (TCO) calculation, ABC-XYZ nomenclature classification, and net present value (NPV) and internal rate of return (IRR) evaluation to substantiate the project's investment viability.
